# Backlink launch pack

Linkable assets on freeplayclassic.com, 25 white-hat targets, and how to pitch them. Each entry lists the page, who would link to it, why, and a ready-to-send note. Send pitches one by one from a real inbox, personalise the first line, and never buy links or swap them in bulk.

## 1. American Mahjong guide & printable scorecard (highest value)

- **Page:** https://freeplayclassic.com/mahjong/american-mahjong-guide/
- **Direct PDF:** https://freeplayclassic.com/downloads/printable-mahjong-scorecard.pdf
- **Old/short URL (redirects):** https://freeplayclassic.com/guides/american-mahjong-cheat-sheet/
- **What it is:** a free one-page reference to the 9 American Mahjong hand categories (2026, 2468, Any Like Numbers, Quints, Consecutive Run, 13579, Winds & Dragons, 369, Singles & Pairs), X vs C hands, the no-joker star, 25–75 point values and who pays, plus a printable scorecard PDF. Links to the free 4-player American Mahjong game for practice.
- **Why people link to it:** teachers and club organisers need a handout for beginners. Printables are the most-linked content type in this niche, and ours has no sign-up, no email gate and no ads.
- **Important:** it does not reproduce the National Mah Jongg League card (the examples are our own) and says so on the page. Never describe it as "the 2026 card" or "the official card" in outreach. Say "guide", "cheat sheet" or "reference".

### Who to contact

| Audience | Where to find them | Angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mahjong clubs and teachers | Club websites, community centre class listings, "learn mahjong" class pages | A free handout for beginner classes; the scorecard prints on one sheet |

| Mahjong bloggers and newsletters | Blogs that review cards, tiles and racks; "how to play" posts | A resource to add to their "beginner resources" list |

| Online groups | Mahjong Facebook groups, subreddits, forums | Share only where the rules allow resource posts; answer questions first |

## 3. "Embed this game" (every game page)

Every game page has a collapsed **Embed this game on your site** box with a copy-ready iframe and a credit link back to the game page. Framed pages show only the game (no header, footer or ads). Offer it to:

- teachers and school sites that want a logic game on a class page;
- library and senior-centre activity pages;
- bloggers writing about a game ("try it right here").

The credit link under the iframe is the backlink; ask partners to keep it.

### Broken-Flash outreach (6)

Flash stopped working in browsers at the end of 2020, and thousands of resource pages still link to dead Flash Mahjong, solitaire, chess and puzzle games. Offering a working, free, ad-light replacement helps the page owner and their readers.

How to find them: search for phrases like `"mahjong" "flash player"`, `"free games" "requires flash"` or `"brain games" seniors flash`, then open the links (or check them in the Wayback Machine) to confirm they are dead.

| # | Target type | Offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | Teacher resource pages with Flash logic or math games | /sudoku/, /puzzles/nonogram/, /board/four-in-a-row/ |
| 21 | Public library "games" pages | /games/ |
| 22 | Senior-centre "brain games" lists | /mahjong/, /solitaire/, /daily/ |
| 23 | Old "best free Mahjong online" posts | /mahjong/ or the matching layout page |
| 24 | Old solitaire and card-game list posts | /solitaire/ and the matching variant |
| 25 | Club or school pages with dead Flash chess or checkers | /board/chess/, /board/checkers/ |

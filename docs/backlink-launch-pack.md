## 1. American Mahjong guide & printable scorecard (highest value)

- **Page:** https://freeplayclassic.com/mahjong/american-mahjong-guide/
- **Direct PDF:** https://freeplayclassic.com/downloads/printable-mahjong-scorecard.pdf
- **Old/short URL (redirects):** https://freeplayclassic.com/guides/american-mahjong-cheat-sheet/
- **What it is:** a free one-page reference to the 9 American Mahjong hand categories (2026, 2468, Any Like Numbers, Quints, Consecutive Run, 13579, Winds & Dragons, 369, Singles & Pairs), X vs C hands, the no-joker star, 25–75 point values and who pays, plus a printable scorecard PDF. Links to the free 4-player American Mahjong game for practice.
- **Why people link to it:** teachers and club organisers need a handout for beginners. Printables are the most-linked content type in this niche, and ours has no sign-up, no email gate and no ads.
- **Important:** it does not reproduce the National Mah Jongg League card (the examples are our own) and says so on the page. Never describe it as "the 2026 card" or "the official card" in outreach. Say "guide", "cheat sheet" or "reference".

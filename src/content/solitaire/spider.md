## Two decks, ten columns

Spider is played with two full decks: 104 cards dealt into ten columns, with the rest held back for five more deals. Unlike Klondike, you can put any card on a card one rank higher regardless of suit, but only **runs of a single suit** move as a group, and only a complete King-to-Ace run of one suit clears.

That tension is the whole game: mixing suits gives you room to manoeuvre now, keeping them pure is what lets you clear later.

## Strategy tips

- **Start with one suit.** One suit teaches the flow of the game: every run you build can eventually clear.
- **Build in suit whenever you can.** When two moves are possible, prefer the one that puts a card on the same suit.
- **Empty a column early.** An empty column is the most powerful tool in Spider: use it to rearrange runs, not just to park a card.
- **Turn over hidden cards before dealing.** Each deal buries every column a little deeper, so expose what you can first.

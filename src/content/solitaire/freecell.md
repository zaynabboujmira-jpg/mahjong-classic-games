## A game of pure skill

In FreeCell every card is dealt face up into eight columns, so there is no luck after the deal: everything you need to know is on the table. Four free cells give you room to dig out buried cards, and a run of cards can move together whenever there is enough free space to shuffle it across one card at a time.

## Strategy tips

- **Plan before you play.** All the cards are visible, so look for the Aces and low cards first and work out how to free them.
- **Keep free cells free.** Each empty cell doubles what you can move later. Fill one only when it uncovers something useful.
- **Empty a column.** An empty column is worth more than a free cell: it doubles the size of the runs you can move.
- **Let the auto-move help.** Cards that can no longer be useful go to the foundations on their own, so you can focus on the hard part.

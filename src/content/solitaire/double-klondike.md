## Klondike, twice the size

Double Klondike keeps everything players love about the classic, the stock, the waste, the hidden cards and the race to the foundations, and doubles it. Two decks shuffled together give 104 cards, nine columns and eight foundations. It is a longer, calmer game with more chances to recover from a bad turn.

## Tips for two decks

- **Turn over hidden cards first.** As in Klondike, a face-down card is the biggest obstacle; prefer moves that reveal one.
- **Use both copies.** When two cards could take a move, pick the one that frees a column or uncovers a hidden card.
- **Do not rush to the foundations.** With eight foundations it is tempting to play everything up, but low cards are useful for building columns.
- **Keep an eye on empty columns.** Only Kings can fill them, so empty one when you have a King ready to move.

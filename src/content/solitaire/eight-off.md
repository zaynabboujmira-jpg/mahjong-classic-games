## FreeCell's patient cousin

Eight Off is an all-open solitaire like FreeCell, so luck plays little part: every card is visible from the start. The difference is in the rules. With eight free cells you have plenty of room to park cards, but columns only build in the same suit and only a King can start a new column, so the game rewards careful planning more than quick shuffling.

## Strategy that wins Eight Off

- **Free the Aces and Twos first.** They go to the foundations as soon as they are uncovered, and every card that goes up frees space.
- **Keep free cells in reserve.** Each empty cell lets you move a longer run; filling all eight usually ends the game.
- **Plan for Kings.** An empty column is only useful for a King, so uncover a King before you empty a column.
- **Work suit by suit.** Building one suit high in a column makes it easy to move up to the foundation later.

## The classic adding-up patience game

Pyramid Solitaire deals 28 cards in a triangle of seven rows, each row overlapping the one above. Remove pairs that total 13 to work your way to the top. Kings come off alone. The remaining 24 cards form a stock you turn over one at a time for more pairs.

## Tips for clearing the pyramid

- **Look ahead before pairing.** When a card can pair with two different cards, choose the one that uncovers more of the pyramid.
- **Clear from the bottom evenly.** Unblocking both sides of the pyramid keeps more cards available at once.
- **Save waste cards.** Pairing two pyramid cards is better than using the waste when either works, because waste cards come round again.
- **Count your passes.** In the 3-passes mode, plan your last trip through the stock carefully.

## The original patience game

Klondike is the solitaire most people learned first. Twenty-eight cards are dealt into seven columns, with only the top card of each face up, and the rest wait in the stock. You win by sorting the whole deck onto four foundations, one per suit, from Ace to King.

The skill lies in **choosing which hidden card to free next**. Every face-down card you turn over gives you new options, so most strong moves are the ones that uncover something.

## Strategy tips

- **Turn over hidden cards first.** When two moves look equal, pick the one that exposes a face-down card, ideally from the tallest column.
- **Don't rush cards to the foundations.** A low card on a foundation is safe, but a 5 or 6 may still be needed to hold a run of the other colour.
- **Empty a column only when a King is ready.** An empty column is useful only if you have a King to put in it.
- **In Draw 3, think about the order.** Playing a card from the waste changes which cards come up on the next pass through the stock.

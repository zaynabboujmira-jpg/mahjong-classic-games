## Solid squares, open edges

The Pyramid is four solid squares stacked like steps. With no gaps in any layer, only the tiles at the **left and right edges of each row** are free at the start, plus whatever sits at the very top.

That makes this a layout about edges. Each match you make at the end of a row exposes the next tile in, and every step you clear from the top exposes a new ring of edges below.

## Strategy tips

- **Take the summit early.** The top block covers the centre of the board. The sooner it goes, the more choices you have.
- **Peel rows evenly.** Clear rows from both ends rather than racing down one side; it keeps more tiles free.
- **Think one layer down.** Before matching, look at what the tile is covering. Prefer matches that uncover tiles you already have partners for.
- **Use flowers and seasons as wildcards.** They match within their group, so they are easy to pair when the edges dry up.

## A layout that rewards patience

The Turtle is built around long horizontal rows. A tile in the middle of a row only becomes free once everything to its left or right is gone, so the real puzzle is deciding **which end of each row to open first**. Rush one side and you can leave matching tiles stranded in the middle.

The shell rises in four steps to a single crowning tile. That tile blocks four tiles below it, so taking it early is almost always a good idea.

## Strategy tips

- **Clear the caps.** The top tile and the three end caps unlock the most tiles for the fewest moves.
- **Work rows from both ends.** Opening a row from the left and the right doubles the number of free tiles in it.
- **Count before you match.** When three of a face are free, think about which two to take: keep the one that is blocking something else for later.
- **Save the seasons and flowers.** Any two flowers match, as do any two seasons, so they make flexible spare moves when you are stuck.

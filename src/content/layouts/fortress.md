## Walls, towers and a keep

The Fortress is a square wall around a courtyard. Each corner holds a tower two layers high with a lookout tile on top, the walls are doubled, and a raised keep stands in the middle of the courtyard.

Long walls mean long blocked rows. The **corners are where the walls begin**, so the towers are your way in: clear a tower and both walls that meet there gain a free end.

## Strategy tips

- **Storm the towers.** Each lookout covers the four tower tiles below it. Remove the lookouts early so the towers, and then the wall ends beside them, can come down.
- **Lower the keep.** The keep sits on top of the courtyard. Once it is gone the courtyard rows open from the ends.
- **Avoid hollowing out one wall.** Clearing a single wall completely can strand tiles behind it in the courtyard. Spread your moves around the square.
- **Hold back a flexible pair.** Keep a flower or season pair available for the moment the walls run dry.

## Wide body, quick start

The Fish is an oval of tiles with a raised back running along its middle and a forked tail fanning out behind it. The rounded body means many rows are short at the top and bottom, so there are lots of free tiles from the first move.

The challenge sits in the **widest rows across the middle**. They are long, and the raised back covers their centre, so they open slowly.

## Strategy tips

- **Lower the back first.** The raised back covers the middle of the long rows. Clear it from the top before working on the body.
- **Open the long rows from both ends.** Both ends of each middle row can be freed, and clearing from both sides halves the work.
- **Keep the fins for later.** The tail fins are easy pairs. Saving a few gives you moves when the body is blocked.
- **Watch the joint.** Two short rows link the tail to the body. Clearing them frees the ends of the body's middle rows.

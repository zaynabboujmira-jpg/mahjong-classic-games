## A ridge to take apart

The Dragon stretches across the whole board: a tail on one side, a horned head on the other and a spine that rises five layers high in the middle. Wings spread above and below the body, and waves of tiles ripple along its sides.

Because the body is long and narrow, the tiles along the spine block each other in chains. The game is about **taking the ridge down evenly** so you never leave a single tall tower whose bottom tile you need.

## Strategy tips

- **Peak first.** The single tile on top of the spine covers four others. Remove it early.
- **Use the head and tail.** Both ends of the body open up quickly and give you free tiles on the lower layers.
- **Keep the wings in reserve.** The wings are short rows with lots of free ends; they are your safety net when the spine runs out of pairs.
- **Watch the waves.** The wave rows hug the body. Clearing them frees the body's sides, but only when the spine above is already lower.

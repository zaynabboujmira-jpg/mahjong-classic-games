## Easy on the eyes, easy to start

The Butterfly is a symmetrical layout: two wings that mirror each other across a stacked body. Each wing narrows towards the middle and carries raised patterns on its upper and lower halves.

The wing rows are short and their outer ends are free from the first move, so you always have plenty of choices. It is the layout to pick for a **calm game**, or to learn how Mahjong Solitaire works.

## Strategy tips

- **Mind the body.** The central column touches the inner end of every wing row. Lowering it early opens both wings from the middle as well as the outside.
- **Match across the wings.** Pairs often sit on opposite wings. Taking one tile from each keeps the butterfly balanced.
- **Clear the raised patterns.** The raised tiles on each wing cover the rows below; removing them first doubles the free ends in those rows.
- **Try for speed.** Because Butterfly flows so well, it is a good layout for chasing a personal best time.

## Accessible by design

Large tiles, a high-contrast face design and keyboard play make the Butterfly a comfortable game on any device.

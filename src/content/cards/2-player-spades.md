## Spades for two

Spades is usually a partnership game for four, but this two-handed version works beautifully head to head. The twist is the draw: instead of a deal, you build your hand one card at a time, keeping some and passing others, so half the deck never comes into play. Then bid, follow suit and trump with spades, exactly as in the four-player game.

## Tips for two-handed Spades

- **Keep spades.** High spades are almost certain tricks, and long spades win the last tricks of the hand.
- **Build a void.** Passing every card of one suit lets you trump it as soon as it is led.
- **Count the cards.** With only 26 cards in play, the cards you discarded tell you what your opponent cannot hold.
- **Bid what you can make.** A set costs 10 per trick bid, but ten bags cost 100, so aim for your real number.

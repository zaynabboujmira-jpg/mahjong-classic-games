## The classic two-player card game with a pegging board

Cribbage is played with a standard 52-card deck and a board of holes used to keep score. Points come in three ways: during the pegging, when cards are laid one at a time towards a count of 31; in the show, when each hand is counted with the starter card; and from the crib, the dealer's extra hand. The first player to peg 121 points wins, even in the middle of a hand.

New to the game? [How to Play Cribbage: Rules and Scoring for Beginners](/blog/how-to-play-cribbage-rules-and-scoring/) explains every step with examples.

## How the cribbage board keeps score

Each player has two pegs and a track of 120 holes, out and back, plus the game hole. The front peg shows your score; the back peg shows where you were before your last score, so you can always see how many points were just pegged. The board above the cards does this automatically, and the toolbar shows the same scores as numbers.

## Tips to score more

- **Keep fives and tens together.** A 5 makes fifteen with every 10, jack, queen and king, so hands built around 5s count big.
- **Feed your own crib, starve theirs.** Throw pairs, fives and cards that make fifteen into your own crib; give the computer's crib cards far apart in rank, such as a king and a 2.
- **Lead a low card.** A 4 or lower can't be answered with a fifteen. Leading a 5 invites a ten-card for 15-2.
- **Avoid a count of 21.** Any ten-card then makes 31 for 2.
- **Save a low card for the go.** Small cards let you squeeze in under 31 and take the point for the go or the last card.

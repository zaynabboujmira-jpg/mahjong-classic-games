## The classic partnership trick-taking game

Spades is played by four players in two teams. Spades are always trumps. Before each round, every player bids the number of tricks they think they can win, and the team then tries to make its combined bid, no more and no less.

## Strategy for winning partnerships

- **Bid honestly.** A set costs 10 points per trick bid, but too many bags cost 100. Aim for the number you can really make.
- **Help your partner.** If your partner is already winning a trick, play low and save your high cards.
- **Cover a Nil.** When your partner bids Nil, play high to win tricks over them and keep them safe.
- **Lead your aces early.** Side-suit aces are surest before someone runs out of the suit and can trump.
- **Count trumps.** There are 13 spades. Knowing how many are left tells you when a high card is safe.

## You, your partner and two opponents

Your partner sits opposite you; West and East play against you. Scores carry over each round until a team reaches 500 points, or falls to −200.

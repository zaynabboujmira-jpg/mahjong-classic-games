## The classic game of avoiding tricks

Hearts is played by four players with a standard 52-card deck. Each hand, everyone tries not to win tricks that contain hearts or the queen of spades. Points are bad: the game ends when someone reaches 100, and the lowest score wins.

## Strategy that keeps your score low

- **Watch the queen of spades.** If you hold it, keep low spades to protect it; if you do not, lead spades to flush it out.
- **Void a suit.** Passing away a short suit lets you discard hearts and the queen when that suit is led.
- **Duck under.** Play the highest card that still loses the trick, to get rid of dangerous high cards safely.
- **Count the hearts.** Keep track of how many hearts have gone; late in a hand a single high heart can be costly.
- **Stop the moon.** If one player has taken every point so far, take a heart yourself to block the shot.

## Three computer opponents

West, North and East are computer players with sensible passing and play. Scores carry over from hand to hand until someone reaches 100.

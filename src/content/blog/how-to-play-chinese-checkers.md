Chinese Checkers is one of the easiest board games to learn and one of the most satisfying to play well. There are no captures and no complicated pieces: just marbles, a star and the thrill of a long chain of hops across the board. This guide explains the setup, the moves, how to win and the tactics that make the difference.

## The board

The board is a **six-pointed star with 121 holes**. In the middle is a hexagon of 61 holes, and each of the six points is a triangle of 10 holes, in rows of 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Each player owns one point at the start, usually marked in their colour, and their **target** is the point directly opposite. The aim is to move every marble from home to the target before anyone else does.

## How to set up for 2 to 6 players

Each player puts **10 marbles** in their starting point, filling all ten holes. Which points you use depends on how many people play:

| Players | Points used | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Two opposite points | Each player's target is the other's start |
| 3 | Every second point | Each target starts empty |
| 4 | Two pairs of opposite points | Two points stay empty |
| 6 | All six points | The busiest game, with crowded traffic in the middle |

Five players is possible but unbalanced, because one player has an empty target and no opponent coming the other way. Most groups avoid it.

## How to move and jump

Players take turns clockwise. On your turn you move **one marble**, in one of two ways.

**A step.** Move the marble to any of the six neighbouring holes, as long as it is empty. That is the whole move.

**A hop.** If a neighbouring hole has a marble in it, of any colour, and the hole directly beyond it in a straight line is empty, you can jump over it into that hole. The marble you hop over stays where it is.

After one hop, the same marble may **keep hopping** from its new hole, over another marble into another empty hole, as many times as you like and in any direction. You can stop the chain whenever you choose. A single chain can carry a marble from one side of the board to the other.

You cannot mix the two kinds of move: a step ends your turn, and once you have hopped you may only hop.

## How to win

The first player to move **all ten marbles into the target point** wins. In a game with more than two players, the others can keep playing for second and third place.

**The blocking rule.** A player could try to stop an opponent by leaving one marble sitting in their own starting point. To prevent this, most rules say that if the target is completely full and at least one of the marbles in it is yours, you win. The game on this site uses this rule.

## Common house rules

Chinese Checkers is played with many small variations. Agree on these before you start:

- **Where marbles may stop.** In the most common rule, a marble may pass through other players' points during a chain of hops but may not end its move there. Some families allow marbles to rest anywhere.
- **Fast or super hops.** In this variant, a marble may hop over another marble from any distance along a line, landing the same distance beyond it. It makes games much quicker.
- **Fifteen marbles.** For two players, some sets use fifteen marbles each, filling the first five rows from the point.

## Winning strategy for beginners

1. **Build ladders.** Line up your marbles with one-hole gaps between them. A marble at the back can then hop along the whole line in one turn.
2. **Use everyone's marbles.** Any marble can be hopped over. Before you move, look for chains through your opponents' pieces too.
3. **Do not leave stragglers.** The game is decided by your last marble. Bring the back of your group forward regularly instead of racing one marble far ahead.
4. **Fill the target from the tip.** When your marbles arrive, move them to the far holes of the point first, so the holes behind stay open for the ones still coming.
5. **Think about the next turn.** A step that sets up a long hop next turn is often better than a short hop now.
6. **Block only when it is cheap.** Standing in a hole an opponent needs can cost them a whole chain, but do not slow your own race to do it.

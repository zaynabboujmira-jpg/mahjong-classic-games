## A bigger, denser field

Intermediate packs 40 mines into a 16×16 board. About one cell in six is a mine, compared with one in eight on Beginner, so openings are smaller and you will spend more time reading the numbers along the edge of what you have opened.

## The patterns that matter here

**1-2 on an edge.** Read a straight wall of numbers from one side. A 1 next to a 2: the 2's extra mine has to be on the cell only it can reach, so flag it.

**1-2-1.** Three numbers in a row against a wall of hidden cells: the mines sit under the two 1s, and the cell under the 2 is safe.

**1-2-2-1.** The mines sit under the two 2s, and the cells under the 1s are safe.

**Subtract shared neighbours.** When two numbers share most of their hidden cells, the difference between their values tells you about the cells only one of them touches.

## Speed tips

- **Chord, do not click.** Once the mines around a number are flagged, one double-click opens the rest. Good players chord far more than they click.
- **Only flag what helps.** You do not need to flag every mine to win; flag the ones you will chord around.
- **Leave guesses for last.** If you are stuck, look for safe progress elsewhere on the board before risking a guess.

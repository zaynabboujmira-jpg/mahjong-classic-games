## The classic challenge

Expert is the board speed-runners compete on: 99 mines on a field 30 cells wide and 16 tall. About one cell in five is a mine, so openings are small and every number counts.

## Expert strategy

**Play the edges systematically.** Work along the border of the opened area, solving each section with the standard patterns (1-1, 1-2, 1-2-1, 1-2-2-1) before moving on.

**Use reduction.** Subtract the flags already placed around a number from its value. A 3 with two flags next to it behaves like a 1, and the familiar patterns appear again.

**Look for shared cells.** When two numbers share hidden neighbours, compare them: if a 2 and a 1 share all but one cell of the 2, that extra cell must be a mine.

**Use the mine counter at the end.** When only a few hidden cells remain, the number of mines left often decides the last cells without a guess.

**Guess smart when you must.** Some positions have no logical answer. Guess early rather than late, and choose a cell that would open new information if it is safe, such as a corner or a cell far from high numbers.

## Speed tips

- **Chord constantly.** Flag only the mines that let you chord, and open everything else with a double-click.
- **Keep your eyes ahead of your mouse.** Find the next safe cell while the current area opens.
- **Use Flag mode on touch screens** if long-press feels slow; switch it off to open cells again.

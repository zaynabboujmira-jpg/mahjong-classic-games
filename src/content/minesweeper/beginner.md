## How Minesweeper works

The field hides 10 mines among 81 cells. Open a cell and it shows a number: how many mines touch it, counting all eight neighbours, diagonals included. Use those numbers to work out which cells are safe and which hide mines. Open every safe cell to win; open a mine and the game is over.

## Your first moves

Your first click is always safe and always opens an area, because mines are placed after it and kept away from it. Start near the middle: a big opening gives you more numbers to work with.

## Patterns every beginner should know

- **A 1 touching one hidden cell.** That cell is a mine. Flag it.
- **A satisfied number.** If a 2 already has two flags around it, all its other neighbours are safe. Double-click it to open them at once.
- **The 1-1 from a wall.** When a straight line of numbers starts at the edge of the board with 1, 1, the first 1's mine is shared with the second, so the hidden cell beyond the second 1 is safe.
- **Count the corners.** A 3 in a corner of an opening often touches exactly three hidden cells, so all three are mines.

## The classic jumping game

Checkers, also called draughts, is played on the dark squares of an 8 × 8 board. Each side starts with twelve pieces. Red moves first. Pieces move diagonally forward and capture by jumping. Reach the far side to crown a king, and win by capturing every enemy piece or blocking them so they cannot move.

## Strategy that wins games

- **Hold the back row.** Keep the pieces on your back row at home as long as you can; they stop your opponent from crowning.
- **Take the centre.** Pieces in the middle of the board have more moves and support each other.
- **Move in pairs.** A piece with a friend behind it cannot be jumped from the front.
- **Use forced jumps.** Because captures are compulsory, you can offer one piece to drag an enemy piece into a double jump of your own.
- **Trade when ahead.** With more pieces, swap one for one to simplify into an easy win.

## Three levels and a two-player mode

Easy is relaxed and good for learning the rules. Medium sees simple traps. Hard plays exchanges to the end and sets traps of its own. Or choose 2 players and play a friend on the same screen.

## The classic vertical strategy game

Four in a Row is played on an upright grid seven columns wide and six rows high. Players take turns dropping a disc into a column, and it falls to the lowest empty space. The first to line up four discs in a row (across, down or diagonally) wins.

## How to win more games

- **Take the centre.** Discs in the middle column can join the most lines. Start there and keep building around it.
- **Make two threats at once.** A move that creates two different rows of three cannot be blocked twice. That is how most games are won.
- **Look under the threat.** Before you drop a disc, check whether it lets your opponent play on top of it to complete a line.
- **Think in rows of three.** Count which empty spaces would finish a line for each player; the player who controls more of the lower ones usually wins the endgame.

## Three computer levels, or a friend

Easy is relaxed and makes mistakes. Medium never misses a direct win or block. Hard thinks seven moves ahead and punishes loose play. Or pick 2 players and play a friend on the same device.

## Noughts and crosses, and its big brother

Tic Tac Toe is the classic pencil-and-paper game: two players take turns marking a 3×3 grid, and three in a row wins. Five in a Row uses the same rules on a 15×15 board with five needed to win, which turns a quick game into a real strategy battle.

## Tips for both games

- **Start in the centre.** The centre square is part of four lines in Tic Tac Toe; in Five in a Row, starting near the centre leaves room in every direction.
- **Block at once.** Two in a row (or an open four in Five in a Row) must be blocked immediately.
- **Build forks.** A move that creates two threats at once cannot be stopped.
- **Mind open threes.** In Five in a Row, three in a row with both ends open becomes an unstoppable open four next turn; block it early.

## Play the computer or a friend

Tic Tac Toe has Easy, Medium and Unbeatable levels; Five in a Row has Easy and Hard. Or play a friend on one device.

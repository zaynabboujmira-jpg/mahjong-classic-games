## The classic tile-matching game

Dominoes is played with 28 tiles, each showing two numbers from blank to six. Players take turns adding a tile to either end of a line, matching the numbers that touch. The first player to play every tile wins the round and scores the pips their opponents are still holding.

## Strategy tips

- **Dump heavy tiles early.** If an opponent goes out, every pip in your hand counts for them. Play 6s and 5s first.
- **Play doubles when you can.** A double fits only one number, so it is easy to get stuck with it later.
- **Keep variety.** A hand with many different numbers can answer more ends.
- **Watch the passes.** When someone passes or draws, they do not have the numbers on the ends. Leave those numbers open to block them.
- **Count the tiles.** Each number appears on seven tiles. If five of the 4s are on the table, the 4s are nearly gone.

## Draw or Block, two to four players

Play the Draw game for longer rounds, or the Block game for a tighter, tactical match. Add up to three computer players for a full table.

## The classic star-board race

Chinese Checkers is played on a six-pointed star with 121 holes. Each player starts with ten marbles in one point of the star and races them across to the point directly opposite. There are no captures: the whole game is about moving faster than everyone else, and the fastest way to move is to hop.

## How to win more games

- **Build ladders.** A line of your own marbles with gaps between them lets a marble at the back hop all the way to the front in one turn.
- **Use other players' marbles.** Any marble can be hopped over, so look for chains through your opponents' pieces too.
- **Do not leave stragglers.** The last marble home decides the game. Bring up the back of your group regularly instead of racing one marble ahead.
- **Fill the far holes first.** When you reach the target, move marbles to the tip of the point first so the holes behind them stay open.
- **Block when it is cheap.** A marble standing where an opponent wants to land can cost them a long hop, but do not delay your own race for it.

## Two to six players

Play a quick head-to-head game against one computer player, a crowded three-, four- or six-player table, or a friend on the same device. The last move is traced on the board, so you can see every chain of hops the computer players make.

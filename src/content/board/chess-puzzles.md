## Train your eye for checkmate

Chess puzzles are the fastest way to get better at chess. Each one is a position where one side can force checkmate, and your job is to find how. Solving a few every day trains you to spot checks, captures and threats, which wins games far more often than memorising openings.

## How to solve a mate puzzle

- **Look at every check first.** Most mates start with a check, because it limits the king's replies.
- **Count the king's escape squares.** A mate needs every square around the king to be covered or blocked.
- **Consider a quiet move.** In a mate in 2, the key move is sometimes not a check but a move that takes away the last escape square or sets up an unstoppable threat.
- **Watch the defender's pieces.** A move that mates only if a defender cannot capture or block is not a mate.

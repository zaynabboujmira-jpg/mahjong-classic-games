## The classic game of strategy

Chess is played on an 8 × 8 board between White and Black, each with sixteen pieces. White moves first. The goal is checkmate: attack the enemy king so it has no safe square. This version knows every rule, shows the legal moves of the piece you pick, and highlights the last move and any check.

## Strategy for every game

- **Control the centre.** Pawns and pieces on the four middle squares reach the most of the board.
- **Develop quickly.** Bring out knights and bishops before moving the same piece twice, and castle early to keep your king safe.
- **Count before you capture.** Check who attacks and defends a square; a pawn is worth 1, a knight or bishop 3, a rook 5 and the queen 9.
- **Look for checks, captures and threats.** Before every move, ask what your opponent's last move attacks, and what your best forcing move is.
- **In the endgame, use your king.** With few pieces left, the king becomes a strong attacker; push passed pawns towards promotion.

## Three computer levels

Start with Beginner to learn the moves, then step up to Club for a fair game. Tactician thinks several moves ahead and sees tactics such as forks and pins, so every piece needs a defender.

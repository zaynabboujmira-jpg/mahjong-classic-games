## Chess without the opening book

Chess960 keeps every rule of chess but shuffles the pieces on the back row before the game starts. The pawns stand where they always do; behind them the rooks, knights, bishops, queen and king take one of 960 arrangements, mirrored for Black. With no memorised openings to lean on, both players think for themselves from move one.

## Tips for random starts

- **Look at the position before moving.** Find which pieces are already active and which are boxed in, especially the bishops.
- **Develop with purpose.** The usual rules still help: control the centre, bring pieces out, and do not move the same piece twice without a reason.
- **Watch for early threats.** Unusual set-ups can leave a pawn or the king exposed from the first move, for example a bishop already aiming at your f-pawn.
- **Plan your castling.** Decide early which side your king will go to, and clear the squares between the king and that rook.

## The classic race and battle game

Backgammon is a two-player game of luck and skill. Each side races fifteen checkers around the board according to the dice, building blocking points and hitting lone checkers on the way, then bears them off. The first player to remove all fifteen wins.

## Strategy that wins games

- **Make points.** Two or more of your checkers on a point make it safe and block your opponent. Points in your home board are the strongest.
- **Avoid blots.** A single checker can be hit and sent back. Take risks only when the gain is worth it, ideally where few rolls can hit you.
- **Build a prime.** Six points in a row cannot be passed. Even four or five in a row trap your opponent's back checkers.
- **Count the race.** When the armies have passed each other, the pip count tells you who is ahead; then simply race home.
- **Use the opening roll well.** Classic openings such as 3-1 (make your 5-point) and 6-1 (make your bar point) are strong starts.

## Three levels and a two-player mode

Learn against Easy, then step up to Medium and Hard. Or choose 2 players and play a friend on the same device.

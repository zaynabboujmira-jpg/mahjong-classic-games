## Simple to learn, hard to master

Flappy Wing is a one-button game: every tap is a flap. Gravity does the rest, and stone columns with narrow gaps scroll towards you. It takes seconds to learn and much longer to get to Platinum.

## Tips for longer runs

- **Find a rhythm.** Small, regular taps keep the bird level; big gaps between taps lead to big drops.
- **Aim for the lower half of a gap.** A flap takes you up quickly, while falling is slower to recover from.
- **Look ahead.** Glance at the next gap as soon as you clear the current one.
- **Stay calm after a medal.** The gap narrows every 10 points, so expect it to get tighter.

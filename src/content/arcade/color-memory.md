## A classic memory challenge

Color Memory tests how much of a sequence you can hold in your head. Four pads (teal, rose, amber and indigo) each have their own note from a C major chord, so you can follow the pattern by eye, by ear, or both. Each round adds one more step to the end, so the challenge grows steadily.

## Tips for a high score

- **Chunk the sequence.** Remember "teal-rose-amber" as one chunk rather than three separate lights.
- **Use the music.** The notes rise from the low teal pad to the high indigo pad, so a sequence often sounds like a little tune.
- **Say it in your head.** Naming the colours or numbers silently as they play makes them stick.
- **Start in Classic.** Three lives let you learn the rhythm before trying Strict or Speed.

## Good for all ages

A round takes seconds, the rules take one sentence to explain, and it works the same with a mouse, a touch screen or a keyboard. That makes it a good warm-up for the brain, a quick break, or a game to play with children.

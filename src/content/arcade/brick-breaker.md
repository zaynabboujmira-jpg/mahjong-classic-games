## The classic wall-smashing arcade game

A wall of coloured bricks, a ball and a paddle. Keep the ball in play, aim it at the bricks, and clear the wall to move on. Brick Breaker adds tougher silver bricks, falling power-ups and five layouts that get faster each time round.

## How to clear walls faster

- **Break through to the top.** Open a gap at the side of the wall and send the ball through it. Behind the wall it bounces between the bricks and the ceiling, clearing many at once.
- **Aim with the edges.** Catch the ball near the end of the paddle to change its direction sharply.
- **Grab M early.** Several balls clear a wall quickly, but watch them all: you only lose a life when the last one falls.
- **Don't chase every capsule.** Keeping the ball in play is worth more than a power-up.

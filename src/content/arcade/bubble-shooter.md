## The classic colour-matching shooter

A cluster of coloured bubbles hangs from the top of the screen. Aim your launcher, fire, and match three or more of the same colour to pop them. Pop the bubbles that hold up a group, and everything below falls for big points.

## Tips for a high score

- **Go for drops.** Popping a few bubbles near the top can bring down a whole cluster underneath.
- **Use the walls.** Bank shots reach gaps you cannot hit directly.
- **Plan with the next bubble.** Swap when the next colour fits better, and save awkward colours for a spot where they will not block.
- **Watch the row counter.** When a new row is about to drop, take a safe shot that pops something.

## Levels that grow

Each level adds rows and, every two levels, a new colour. New rows arrive sooner on higher levels, so clear quickly and keep the cluster away from the line.

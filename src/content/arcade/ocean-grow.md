## Big fish, little fish

Ocean Grow is a relaxed game about one simple rule: you can eat anything smaller than you, and anything bigger can eat you. Every fish you eat makes you a little bigger, turning yesterday's predators into today's lunch.

## Tips for growing fast

- **Start with the small ones.** Early on, most of the ocean is dangerous; pick off tiny fish near the edges.
- **Watch the outlines.** A red outline means danger. When it disappears, that fish is fair game.
- **Keep moving gently.** Big fish swim slowly in straight lines. Stay out of their path and let them pass.
- **Mind the edges.** New fish enter from the sides, sometimes big ones. Do not linger at the very edge.

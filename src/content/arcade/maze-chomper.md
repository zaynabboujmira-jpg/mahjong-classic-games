## A maze chase with four hunters

Maze Chomper is a fast maze chase. Steer the Chomper through the corridors, eat every pellet, and stay one step ahead of the four Wisps. Each Wisp hunts in its own way, so the trick is to read them: the one behind you is rarely the dangerous one.

## Tips for a high score

- **Plan a route.** Clear long corridors in one sweep and leave single pellets near the den for last.
- **Save the power orbs.** Eat one when several Wisps are close, then chase them while they are pale.
- **Watch Scout and Twist.** They try to get in front of you, so turn early at junctions.
- **Use the tunnel.** Wisps chasing you through the side tunnel lose ground as you come out on the far side.
- **Grab the gem.** It is worth far more than a pellet and only stays for nine seconds.

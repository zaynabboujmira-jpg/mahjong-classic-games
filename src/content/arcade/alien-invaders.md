## Hold the line

A fleet of aliens marches side to side, stepping down every time it reaches an edge. You have one cannon, one shot at a time and four shields that crumble as they take hits. The fleet speeds up as it shrinks, and every wave starts lower.

## Tips for surviving longer

- **Thin out the edges.** Clearing a whole column at the side makes the fleet travel further before it steps down, buying you time.
- **Shoot from under a shield.** Fire through a gap you have opened in your own shield; their bombs still have to get through the rest.
- **Watch the bombs.** Aliens often drop bombs from the column right above you. Keep moving.
- **Go for the saucer when it is safe.** It is worth up to 300 points, but do not get caught in the open.

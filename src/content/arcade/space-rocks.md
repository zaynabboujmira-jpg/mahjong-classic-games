## Inertia is the whole game

Space Rocks is about momentum. Your ship turns quickly but keeps sliding in the direction it was going, and every rock, shot and the ship itself wraps round the edges of the screen. Big rocks are slow and easy to hit; every hit splits them into faster, smaller pieces.

## Tips for a high score

- **Stay near the centre.** You can react to rocks coming from any edge, and the screen wrap cannot surprise you as easily.
- **Use short bursts of thrust.** Long thrusts leave you drifting too fast to stop.
- **Break rocks one at a time.** Splitting every large rock at once fills the screen with fast fragments.
- **Save hyperspace.** Use it when you are boxed in, not as your first escape.

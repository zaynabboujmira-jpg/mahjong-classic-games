## A dangerous commute

Five empty burrows wait at the top of the screen. Between you and them lie five lanes of traffic, a strip of bank and five lanes of river. Cars and trucks move at different speeds in each lane; on the river you must ride logs and turtles, which carry you sideways as you go.

## Tips for crossing safely

- **Pause on the bank.** The strip between the road and the river is safe: stop there and plan your river crossing.
- **Watch the turtles.** A flashing group is about to dive. Long logs are the safest rides.
- **Let the river carry you.** Ride a log sideways until a gap opens above you rather than waiting in one spot.
- **Fill the outer burrows first.** The middle burrow is the easiest to reach, so keep it for when the timer is short.

## The original video game sport

Two paddles, one ball, and a simple goal: get the ball past your opponent. Paddle Ball keeps the rules everyone knows and adds what older versions lack: smooth play on any screen, touch controls, three computer levels and a two-player mode.

## How to win rallies

- **Aim with the edges.** The farther from the middle you hit, the steeper the return. A steep shot bounces off the walls and is harder to reach.
- **Return to the centre.** After each hit, move back towards the middle so you can reach either corner.
- **Watch the speed.** The ball gets faster with every hit, so long rallies reward early positioning more than quick reactions.
- **Play the angles against the computer.** Easy and Medium can be beaten with steep shots; Unbeatable cannot, so try to keep the rally going as long as you can.

## Retro sound, modern play

The bleeps are generated in your browser, with no audio files to download, and can be switched off. The game runs at your screen's refresh rate and pauses automatically when you leave the tab.

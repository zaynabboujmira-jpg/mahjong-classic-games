## The original grow-and-dodge game

Snake has one rule: keep eating and never bite yourself. Every berry makes you longer, so the field fills up with your own tail and every turn needs more planning. It is the game that made phones fun, and it still works best in short, focused bursts.

## Tips for a longer snake

- **Hug the edges early.** In Walls mode, run along the edge in wide loops so your tail stays out of the middle.
- **Leave yourself a way out.** Before you head for a berry, check you can turn away again afterwards.
- **Zig-zag when long.** A long snake is safest moving in tidy back-and-forth rows, which leaves the most open space.
- **Use Wrap to escape.** In Wrap mode, going through an edge is often the safest way out of a tight spot.

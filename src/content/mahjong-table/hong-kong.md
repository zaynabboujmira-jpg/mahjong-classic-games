## Hong Kong Mahjong in brief

Hong Kong (Cantonese) Mahjong is the most widely played four-player style. It uses 144 tiles: three suits of 1 to 9, four winds, three dragons, and eight flowers and seasons as bonus tiles. You win with four sets and a pair, and the hand is scored in **faan**. This table plays the common house rule that a hand needs **at least 3 faan** to win.

You sit at the bottom, East is the dealer, and play moves counter-clockwise: the player on your right goes after you, the player on your left before you. That is why you can only chow the discard of the player on your left.

## Getting to 3 faan

- **Half flush (3).** One suit plus winds and dragons. The most reliable route to the minimum.
- **All pungs (3).** Four pungs or kongs and a pair. Calling pungs freely is fine for this hand.
- **Dragons and winds (1 each).** A pung of any dragon, your seat wind or the round wind adds a faan.
- **Concealed and self-drawn (1 each).** Winning without calling any sets, or drawing the winning tile yourself, both count.

## Strategy tips

- **Decide early.** If eight or more of your tiles are in one suit, go for the flush and discard the other suits.
- **Do not chow too much.** Each chow opens your hand and loses the concealed faan; chows only pay off when they help a flush.
- **Keep valuable pairs.** A pair of dragons or of your wind is one call away from a faan.
- **Defend late.** When a player has three sets exposed, they are close: discard tiles that have already been discarded.

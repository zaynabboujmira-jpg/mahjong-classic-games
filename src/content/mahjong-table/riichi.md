## Mahjong the Japanese way

Riichi is the most popular way to play Mahjong in Japan and online. It uses the same 136 tiles and the same four-sets-and-a-pair shape as Chinese rules, but adds the ideas that make it distinctive: every win needs a yaku, a ready closed hand can declare riichi, dora tiles add value, and furiten stops you from winning on a tile you threw away yourself.

## Tips for your first Riichi games

- **Keep your hand closed.** A closed hand can always declare riichi, the easiest yaku.
- **Go for tanyao.** All simples (no 1s, 9s or honours) is a yaku even with open calls.
- **Call only value honours early.** A pung of dragons, your seat wind or the round wind gives a yaku straight away.
- **Watch your discards.** If a tile you need is in your own discards, you are furiten and can only win by tsumo.

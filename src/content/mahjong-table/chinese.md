## The easiest way into Mahjong

Classic Chinese Mahjong is played with 136 tiles: Dots, Bamboo and Characters from 1 to 9, the four winds and the three dragons, four of each. Every player starts with 13 tiles, the dealer with 14, and the first to complete **four sets and a pair** wins.

Because there is no minimum score here, any complete hand is a win. That makes it the best table for learning: focus on building sets, and let the scoring come later.

## Sets you can build

- **Chow:** three tiles in a row in one suit, such as 4-5-6 of Bamboo. Only from your own tiles or the left player's discard.
- **Pung:** three identical tiles. Call it from any discard.
- **Kong:** four identical tiles. Declare it from your hand or call it from a discard, then draw a replacement tile.
- **Pair:** two identical tiles, the "eyes" of the hand.

## Strategy tips

- **Get rid of loners first.** Single winds and dragons that nobody pairs are rarely useful; discard them early.
- **Keep connected tiles.** 3-4 or 5-7 of the same suit can become a chow in more ways than two unrelated tiles.
- **Count what is left.** Tiles already discarded or exposed will not come back, so do not wait for them.
- **Use the hint.** It shows the discard that keeps your hand closest to complete with the most ways to improve.

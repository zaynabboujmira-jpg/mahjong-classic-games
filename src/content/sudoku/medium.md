## The step up from easy

Medium puzzles give you fewer clues, about 30, and they are built so that looking cell by cell is no longer enough. Somewhere in every medium puzzle you will reach a point where no cell has a single option left. That is where **hidden singles** come in.

## Hidden singles, step by step

Instead of asking "which digit fits this cell?", ask "where can this digit go in this box?"

1. Choose a box and a digit it is still missing, say 5.
2. Look along the rows and columns that pass through the box. Every 5 already placed there rules out a whole line of cells.
3. If only one empty cell in the box is left for the 5, that is where it goes.

The same works for a row or a column. People usually find hidden singles faster than naked singles once they get used to them, because you can sweep one digit across the whole grid.

## Strategy tips

- **Sweep one digit at a time.** Pick a digit and check all nine boxes for it before moving on.
- **Start with the digits you see most.** A digit with seven or eight copies placed has very few spots left.
- **Use Fill notes for a full picture.** It pencils every candidate into every empty cell, and notes clear automatically as you place digits.

## The top level

Expert puzzles are built to need at least one **advanced pattern**: an X-Wing, an XY-Wing or a Swordfish. They are still fully logical. Every puzzle is solved step by step by the same engine that powers the hints before you see it, so there is always a way forward without guessing.

## The three expert techniques

**X-Wing.** Pick a digit and look at the rows. If the digit has exactly two possible cells in row A and exactly two in row B, and they line up in the same two columns, the digit fills two diagonal corners of that rectangle. Remove it from every other cell in those two columns. The same applies with rows and columns swapped.

**Swordfish.** An X-Wing across three rows and three columns. Each row has the digit in two or three places, all inside the same three columns. Remove the digit from the rest of those columns.

**XY-Wing.** Find a pivot cell with exactly two candidates, X and Y. Find two cells it can see: one with X and Z, one with Y and Z. Whatever the pivot turns out to be, one of those two cells must be Z, so any cell that sees both of them cannot be Z.

## Strategy tips

- **Keep your notes accurate.** A single missing candidate can hide or fake a pattern. Fill notes resets everything from the current grid.
- **Hunt one digit at a time for fish.** Highlight a digit by tapping it in the grid and look for rows where it appears exactly twice.
- **Look for cells with two candidates.** They are the raw material of XY-Wings.
- **Use the hint to learn.** It shows the next cell you can fill and names the technique that unlocks it, so you can go back and find the pattern yourself.

## Short words, sharp thinking

Four-letter words are everywhere, which is exactly what makes them tricky: once you have three letters, there may be five or six words that fit. Good four-letter play is about **choosing guesses that split the remaining options**, not just guessing the most likely word.

## Tips for four letters

- **Start with a vowel-rich word** like ROAD, LANE or TIME to find the vowel quickly.
- **Watch for families.** If you have _ANE, the answer could be CANE, LANE, MANE, PANE, SANE or WANE. Guess a word that tests several of those first letters at once.
- **Remember endings.** Many four-letter words end in E, K, L, T or Y.

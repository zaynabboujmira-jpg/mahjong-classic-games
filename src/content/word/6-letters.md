## A bigger word, a bigger puzzle

With six letters, a single guess can reveal a lot, but the space of possible answers is much larger. The best players treat the first two guesses as **scouting**: cover as many common letters as possible, then start placing them.

## Tips for six letters

- **Scout with two different words.** A pair like STOLEN and CHAIRS tests eleven common letters in two guesses.
- **Think in chunks.** Endings like -ER, -LE, -ON and -ET, and pairs like CH, SH, TH and ST, are far more common than single letters suggest.
- **Use the keyboard.** The coloured keys show what is left; build candidates only from letters that are still possible.

## How the game works

A hidden five-letter word is waiting. Type a guess, press Enter, and every letter is marked:

- **Green**: right letter, right spot.
- **Orange**: the letter is in the word, but in another spot.
- **Grey**: the letter is not in the word.

You have six guesses. The keyboard keeps a running record of every letter you have tried, so you can see at a glance what is left.

## Strategy that works

- **Open with common letters.** Words that use E, A, R, O, T, L, I and S, such as STARE or SLATE, rule out or confirm several frequent letters at once.
- **Do not reuse grey letters.** Every guess should test something new until you are close.
- **Mind the doubles.** Words like APPLE, GEESE and SKILL repeat letters. If you are stuck, consider a repeated letter.
- **Think in patterns.** With the _RA_E pattern, list candidates (GRAPE, CRANE, BRAVE, GRADE) and pick the guess that splits them best.

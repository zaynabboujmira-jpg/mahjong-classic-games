## A word game in a circle of letters

Seven letters sit on a wheel. Find as many words as you can hidden in them: tap, drag or type, then submit. Every wheel hides one word that uses all seven letters, worth a big bonus.

## Tips for finding more words

- **Start with endings.** Look for common endings such as -ER, -ED, -ING and -EST, then add letters in front.
- **Try plurals.** If there is an S, many four- and five-letter words also work with S added.
- **Shuffle often.** Seeing the letters in a new order brings new words to mind.
- **Hunt for the big one.** Finding the seven-letter word early gives a big boost and often suggests shorter words.

Play the daily wheel to compare with friends, then keep going with unlimited new wheels. Your best score is saved in your browser.

## The classic pairs game

All the cards start face down. Turn two over: matching symbols stay up, different ones turn back. The fewer moves you need to uncover every pair, the better your memory has worked. The symbols are drawn in your browser: suns, moons, leaves, fish and more, each with its own colour.

## How to use fewer moves

- **Turn a new card first.** When you are unsure, the first card of a move should be one you have not seen. If it matches something you remember, you get the pair for free.
- **Sweep in order.** Turning cards row by row makes their positions easier to remember than jumping around.
- **Say it in your head.** Naming the symbol and its place ("moon, top left") helps the memory stick.
- **Don't rush.** Time only breaks ties; the move count is your score.

## Three sizes, one goal

Start on the 4×3 board, move to the classic 4×4, and take on 6×5 when you want a challenge with fifteen pairs.

## The relaxing word game

A word search hides a list of words in a grid of letters. Find each one and drag across it to mark it. Every puzzle here is built for you on the spot from a theme you pick, so there is always a new grid to solve.

## Tips for finding words faster

- **Hunt for rare letters.** Words with Z, Q, X, J or K are easy to spot; look for those letters first.
- **Scan for the first two letters.** Find the first letter, then check the eight squares around it for the second.
- **Go row by row.** Sweep each row, then each column, then the diagonals, instead of jumping around.
- **Watch for doubles.** Double letters such as the "oo" in "moose" stand out in a grid.

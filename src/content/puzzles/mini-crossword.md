## A crossword in a coffee break

A mini crossword is a small grid with a handful of words that cross each other. Read a clue, type the answer and use the crossing letters to crack the harder ones. Each grid here is built from a list of everyday words with original clues.

## Tips for solving

- **Start with the short words.** Three-letter answers are quick to get and give you letters for the longer ones.
- **Use the crossings.** If a clue has you stuck, fill the words that cross it first.
- **Think about the clue's form.** A clue ending in "-ing" or written in the plural usually has an answer in the same form.
- **Check sparingly.** One Check after a first pass shows which guesses to rethink, without giving answers away.

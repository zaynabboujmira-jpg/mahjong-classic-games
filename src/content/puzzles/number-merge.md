## A sliding number puzzle with simple rules

Every move slides all the tiles at once. Equal numbers that meet join into one tile with their sum: two 2s make a 4, two 4s make an 8, and so on up to 2048. A new tile appears after each move, so the board slowly fills up. The skill is keeping it tidy enough to keep merging.

## Tips for a high score

- **Pick a corner.** Keep your highest tile in one corner and never let it leave.
- **Build a chain.** Line up the next numbers along the edge beside it: 1024, 512, 256… so one swipe can merge them all.
- **Use three directions.** Avoid the direction that would pull your corner tile out. When you must use it, undo can rescue a bad swipe.
- **Merge small tiles early.** Lots of 2s and 4s fill the board fast; join them before they block the chain.

## Three boards

The 4×4 board is the classic. Try 3×3 for a short, tense game where every cell counts, or 5×5 when you want a long, relaxed session chasing 4096 and beyond.
